This session addresses the critical aspects of risk management and ethics in generative AI, focusing on data privacy, security, and intellectual property concerns. It covers ethical issues, including managing bias, preventing misinformation (AI “hallucinations”), and ensuring accountability in AI applications. The session also discusses relevant legal frameworks and regulatory considerations for businesses deploying generative AI, highlighting the importance of responsible and compliant AI integration.
