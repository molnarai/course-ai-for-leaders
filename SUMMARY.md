# Artificial Intelligence for Leaders

The objective of this course is to prepare participants with a solid understanding of Generative AI’s transformative potential and its implications for shaping the future of business and society.

This course offers an immersive introduction to Generative Artificial Intelligence (AI) and its implications for modern businesses. Participants will delve into the historical context and fundamental concepts of (Generative) AI, exploring its evolution and impact on various industries. Through interactive discussions, case studies, and hands-on activities, students will gain a comprehensive understanding of key concepts such as neural networks, generative models, and the practical applications of transfer learning and prompt engineering.

Throughout the course, participants will explore the diverse applications of Generative AI, including creative content generation, natural language processing, and healthcare advancements, while analyzing real-world case studies to understand implementation challenges and ethical considerations. Engaging in group activities and discussions, students will refine their problem-solving skills, foster collaboration, and explore strategies for integrating Generative AI into existing business frameworks. Additionally, discussions on ethical practices and responsible AI will underscore the importance of transparency and fairness in AI-driven decision-making processes.

## Session 1: Intro Generative AI

The course opens with an introduction to Generative AI, placing today's tools in the broader history of artificial intelligence and explaining the fundamentals of how AI systems are developed. Participants learn what distinguishes generative models from earlier forms of AI and why their capabilities have advanced so quickly. The session also gives an overview of prompt engineering techniques for generating text and images, showing how the way a request is framed shapes the quality and usefulness of the output. Together, these topics lay the foundation for understanding what Generative AI can and cannot do, and where it creates value in practice.

## Session 2: Agentic AI Systems

This session moves from individual generative models to AI systems that combine Generative AI with other functions, such as tools, data sources, and automated workflows. Participants learn the concepts behind agentic AI systems, in which a model can plan steps, take actions, and work toward a goal rather than simply answer a single prompt. The session introduces the tools used to build such systems and gives participants direct, hands-on experience assembling one, so that they understand both the potential and the practical limits of agentic AI.

## Session 3: Strategic Applications of Generative AI in Business

This session examines the strategic uses of Generative AI in business, focusing on high-impact opportunities in functions such as marketing, customer service, and product development. Case studies of successful AI-driven transformations in sectors such as healthcare, finance, and retail illustrate how organizations have turned these opportunities into real-world value. The session also presents frameworks for deciding whether to build or buy Generative AI solutions, giving participants clear criteria for evaluating which approach best fits their organization's needs, capabilities, and goals.

## Session 4: Operationalizing Generative AI – Integration and Customization

This session turns to the practical work of integrating Generative AI into existing enterprise systems and workflows, emphasizing the steps needed to embed AI smoothly within established structures. Participants weigh the choice between customizing generative models for specific business needs and adopting readily available tools, considering the costs, risks, and benefits of each path. The session also explores the role of data feedback loops, showing how the ongoing integration of new data refines model accuracy and effectiveness and allows AI performance to improve continuously over time.

## Session 5: Risk Management and Ethical Considerations in Generative AI

This session addresses the risks and ethical questions that come with deploying Generative AI, beginning with concerns about data privacy, security, and intellectual property. Participants examine ethical issues such as managing bias, preventing misinformation caused by AI "hallucinations," and ensuring clear accountability for AI-driven outcomes. The session also reviews the legal frameworks and regulatory considerations that businesses must account for, underscoring why responsible and compliant integration is essential to using Generative AI successfully and sustaining the trust of customers, employees, and regulators.

## Session 6: Leading Generative AI Transformation in Organizations

The final session focuses on the leadership strategies needed to promote Generative AI adoption within organizations and to drive AI initiatives across teams and departments. Participants explore why cross-functional teams are essential to the success of AI projects and how to build them. The session details change management techniques for securing organizational buy-in, helping leaders manage a smooth transition and build broad support for Generative AI transformation efforts.
